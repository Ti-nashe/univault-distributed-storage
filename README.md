 1. Research Question
How can the OpenAlex dataset be used to support the organisation, management and discovery of academic research information through the proposed UniVault platform?

 2. Project Aim
To investigate how the OpenAlex dataset can be utilised to support the organisation and retrieval of academic research information through UniVault.

3. Objectives:

1. Examine the structure and content of the OpenAlex dataset.
2. Identify relevant information such as publications, authors and institutions.
3. Investigate ways of organising and retrieving academic research information.
4. Propose how UniVault can improve access to academic research information.
5. Identify the potential benefits and limitations of the proposed platform.

4. Dataset

The project will use **OpenAlex**, an open catalogue of scholarly information. It provides information about research works, authors, institutions, sources and topics.

- OpenAlex website: https://openalex.org/
- OpenAlex API documentation: https://docs.openalex.org/

5. Research Plan
The group will:

1. Identify the problem of accessing and organising academic research information.
2. Explore the structure of the OpenAlex dataset.
3. Identify relevant records from the dataset.
4. Investigate how the information can be organised and retrieved.
5. Propose an approach for the UniVault platform.
6. Evaluate the possible benefits and limitations.
7. Document the findings.
